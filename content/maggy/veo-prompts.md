# Maggy: Veo prompts for all 10 videos

Each video is two Veo clips joined in an editor such as CapCut:

| Clip | Beats | Keep | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 1 and 2 | first ~7s | The problem, then she holds up the Phrase Card |
| **B** | 3 | first ~3s | The payoff line and her satisfied reaction |

Veo generates 8-second clips. Trim A to about 7 seconds and B to about 3.

## Before you start

1. **Make a reference image first.** Generate one still of Maggy using the prompt below. In Flow, add it as an *ingredient* for every clip so her face stays the same across all 20 clips.
2. **Set the aspect ratio to 9:16 (portrait)** in Flow's settings, as well as in the prompt.
3. **The card and the laptop screen stay blank on purpose.** Veo writes text badly and often scrambles it. Add the Phrase Card text and the result graphic in your editor. The overlay text for each video is listed with its prompts.
4. **Spoken lines are spelled the way they should sound.** For example, "E-L-I-five" makes Veo say the letters instead of "Eli". If a word still comes out wrong, re-spell it and generate again.
5. **Generate 2–4 takes of each clip** and keep the best one. Lip sync and hand movements vary between takes.

## If a take goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Scrambled text on the card or screen | Add "the card is completely blank" or "the screen is not visible" |
| Veo adds its own subtitles | Move "No subtitles, no captions" to the very start of the prompt |
| She says "Eli five" | Spell it with spaces: "E. L. I. five" |
| Line is cut off at 8 seconds | Remove a few words from the line, or add "she speaks briskly" |
| Her face drifts between clips | Use the reference image as an ingredient, and keep the character block word for word |
| Wrong cardigan colour | Add "mustard-yellow cardigan" a second time, in the action paragraph |

# B-roll version: text over background footage

Any video can also be made without dialogue: B-roll plays in the background and the three beats appear as text on top.

**Which format to use:**

| Format | Best for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Maggy talking (above) | **1, 2, 3, 4, 7** | Short phrases, and her personality carries them |
| Text over B-roll (below) | **5, 6, 8, 9, 10** | Long phrases are easier to read than to hear |

Every video has text beats below, so you can use either format for any of them.

**How to make it:**
- **One 8s clip per video.** Slow it to 80% speed to get 10 seconds, or hold the last frame for 2 seconds.
- **Text timing:** Beat 1 at 0–3s, Beat 2 (the phrase) at 3–7s, Beat 3 at 7–10s. Keep each beat under about 8 words, except the phrase itself.
- **Where to put text:** in the top half or centre. Keep it out of the bottom 20% and the right edge, where the app's buttons and captions sit.
- **Sound:** Veo gives you room tone. Add music or a trending sound in your editor.
- **Seven clips cover all ten videos.** Generate them once and reuse them.
