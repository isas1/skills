# Maggy: profile and 10 short videos

Every tip below comes from this repo's skills (`eli5-succinct` and `summary`), cut down to a phrase someone can type in 10 seconds.

---

## 1. Refined profile

### 👤 Character identity

- **Name:** Margaret ("Maggy")
- **Age:** 55
- **Archetype:** The Digital Organiser. She's the one who has always run the club rota, and now she makes AI do the dull bits.
- **Audience:** Adults 50+ who aren't technical. They use a phone and email and have tried ChatGPT or Claude once or twice, and they found it waffly, confusing or a bit patronising.
- **Promise:** *"Say the right words and AI talks to you like a sensible assistant."*
- **Catchphrase (sign-off):** *"Copy it, paste it, put the kettle on."*
- **Transparency:** Maggy is an AI character, and her bio says so ("AI host, real tips"). TikTok, YouTube and Meta all require a label on realistic AI-generated people. With a 50+ audience, being upfront also earns trust.

### 🎨 Visual and aesthetic (Flow/Veo)

Paste this character block, word for word, at the start of every Veo prompt so she looks the same in every clip:

> Maggy, a 55-year-old woman with a sleek silver chin-length bob and tortoiseshell reading glasses, wearing a mustard-yellow cardigan over a white blouse. She sits at a tidy oak desk in a bright, sunlit home office with colour-coded binders on the shelf behind her and a cup of tea in a floral mug. Warm natural light, medium close-up, eye level, steady camera. Sharp, witty, reassuring expression.

- **Signature prop, the Phrase Card:** a handwritten index card that Maggy holds up in Beat 2 of every video. It shows the exact words to type, and the same words appear as on-screen text. It becomes her visual trademark and matches the "neat binders" look.
- **One cardigan colour**, always. Changing colours makes Veo drift and viewers stop recognising her.

### 📱 Content strategy

- **Niche:** Life admin made easy, with AI.
- **Core angle:** *"You don't need to learn AI. You need the magic phrase."* Each video teaches one phrase.
- **Topics (the settings where a phrase gets used):** club events and rotas, family spreadsheets, letters from the bank or council, long email threads, insurance renewals, replying to people.
- **Tone:** Friendly, practical, dry wit, zero jargon. If a term like "ELI5" can't be avoided, she explains it in the same breath.

**The 3-beat format (10 seconds, about 25 spoken words):**

| Beat | Time | Job | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The Grumble | 0–3s | Name the pain | Maggy reacts to a mess on screen |
| 2. The Phrase | 3–7s | Give the exact words | She holds up the Phrase Card |
| 3. The Payoff | 7–10s | Show the result and sign off | A tidy answer on screen, she sips her tea |

Veo clips run about 8 seconds, so generate Beats 1 and 2 as one clip. Then make Beat 3 a 2–3 second end card: the result on screen, the Phrase Card and "link in bio".

### 💰 Monetisation and funnel

- **Hook:** "AI gives me waffle, jargon or a lecture."
- **CTA:** *"Grab my free phrase card, link in bio."* (Say "phrase card", not "command cheat sheet". "Command" sounds technical.)
- **Lead magnet (free):** one page with the 10 phrases from the videos below.
- **Paid product:** *The Magic Phrase Book: Copy-and-Paste AI Phrases for Life Admin (50+)*, a PDF on Stan Store.
  - ⚠️ **Change from the original profile:** it described a "Claude Code command library". Claude Code is a developer tool that runs in a terminal, which is the opposite of zero jargon for this audience. Everything in these videos works by typing into the normal Claude or ChatGPT app, and the product should promise that too. A Claude Code edition could be a later "Level 2" upsell, if at all.

## 2. Skills pulled from the repo (10-second versions)

| Repo skill | What it does | Maggy's 10-second phrase |
|---|---|---|
| `eli5-succinct` | Plain explanation, short | **"ELI5 succinct."** |
| `eli5-succinct`: number is an audience dial | ELI18 = clever adult, new to the topic | **"ELI18."** (explains without talking down) |
| README one-liner | Shrink what was just said | **"TL;DR, plain English."** |
| `eli5-succinct`: yes/no | Verdict first, detail second | **"Yes or no?"** then **"Explain."** |
| `eli5-succinct`: say what you don't know. `summary`: never invent numbers | Stops confident guessing | **"If you're not sure, say so. Don't guess."** |
| `summary` accessible mode: next step boxed at the top | Action first | **"What's the one thing I need to do? Put it first."** |
| `summary` accessible mode: one idea per block, dyslexia rules | Easy to read | **"One idea per line. Short lines. No jargon."** |
| `eli5-succinct` / `summary`: write in the reader's own language | Matches your voice | **"Reply in my style. Here's how I write:"** |
| `summary` variant: summary of the changes | Before and after | **"Compare these. Show me only what changed."** |
| `summary` house-style prompt | One visual page instead of a wall of text | **"Make this a one-page visual summary. Pictures over paragraphs."** |

## 3. Ten videos

Each one: 🎣 Beat 1 (0–3s) · 💡 Beat 2 (3–7s), Phrase Card · ✅ Beat 3 (7–10s)

### 1. "Two Magic Words" (ELI5 succinct)
- 🎣 *Squints at a wall of text on her laptop.* "Does AI answer you like a textbook?"
- 💡 *Card: **ELI5 SUCCINCT**.* "Add two words: ELI5 succinct."
- ✅ *Three tidy lines on screen, she sips her tea.* "Explain like I'm five, briefly. Lovely."

### 2. "I'm Not Five, Dear" (ELI18)
- 🎣 *Raises an eyebrow at the screen.* "AI talking to you like a toddler?"
- 💡 *Card: **ELI18**.* "Type ELI18. That's 'explain like I'm eighteen'."
- ✅ *Nods, satisfied.* "Clever adult, new to the topic. Much better."

### 3. "The 40-Email Thread" (TL;DR, plain English)
- 🎣 *Scrolls an endless email chain about the summer fête.* "Forty emails about one fête?"
- 💡 *Card: **TL;DR, PLAIN ENGLISH**.* "Paste them in. Type: TL;DR, plain English."
- ✅ *Four bullet points on screen.* "Who, what, when. Done before the kettle boils."

### 4. "Just Tell Me Yes or No" (verdict first)
- 🎣 *Holds up a council letter, baffled.* "Do I even need to reply to this?"
- 💡 *Card: **…YES OR NO?** then **EXPLAIN.*** "Ask, then add: yes or no?"
- ✅ *Screen shows "No." in large type.* "Answer first. Type 'explain' only if you care."

### 5. "Stop Guessing" (honesty)
- 🎣 *Frowns.* "AI sounds very sure… and gets it wrong."
- 💡 *Card: **IF YOU'RE NOT SURE, SAY SO. DON'T GUESS.*** "Add this line to any question."
- ✅ *Screen: "I'm not certain, so check your provider."* "Honest beats confident."

### 6. "The One Thing" (action first)
- 🎣 *Drops a three-page bank letter on the desk.* "Three pages. What do they actually want?"
- 💡 *Card: **WHAT'S THE ONE THING I NEED TO DO? PUT IT FIRST.*** "Paste the letter. Ask this."
- ✅ *One boxed line on screen: "Sign and return by 14 Oct."* "One job. Sorted."

### 7. "Easy on the Eyes" (readable output)
- 🎣 *Pushes her glasses up and squints.* "Tiny paragraphs of fog?"
- 💡 *Card: **ONE IDEA PER LINE. SHORT LINES. NO JARGON.*** "Tell it how you like to read."
- ✅ *Clean, spaced-out steps on screen.* "My eyes thank me."

### 8. "Sound Like Me" (your own voice)
- 🎣 *Reads an AI reply aloud, deadpan.* "'Dear valued neighbour'? I'd never say that."
- 💡 *Card: **REPLY IN MY STYLE. HERE'S HOW I WRITE:*** "Paste two emails you've written."
- ✅ *Warm, short reply on screen, she smiles.* "Now it sounds like me."

### 9. "Spot the Difference" (before and after)
- 🎣 *Holds two insurance renewals side by side.* "Last year versus this year. What changed?"
- 💡 *Card: **COMPARE THESE. SHOW ME ONLY WHAT CHANGED.*** "Paste both. Ask this."
- ✅ *Two columns on screen: "£312 → £389".* "Found the sneaky bit."

### 10. "One Page, Not Ten" (visual summary)
- 🎣 *Lifts a thick stack of AGM minutes.* "Twelve pages of meeting minutes?"
- 💡 *Card: **MAKE THIS A ONE-PAGE VISUAL SUMMARY. PICTURES OVER PARAGRAPHS.*** "Paste it in. Ask this."
- ✅ *A tidy one-pager with a simple chart.* "Pin it to the club noticeboard."

**Every video closes on the same end card:** Phrase Card + "Free phrase card, link in bio" + *"Copy it, paste it, put the kettle on."*

**Suggested posting order:** 1 → 3 → 6 → 4 → 5 → 2 → 8 → 9 → 7 → 10. Start with the easiest win and save the "wow" visual one for last. Number 2 works best after number 1 has landed.
